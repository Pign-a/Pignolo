# KPI card
Use when: one number answers a daily question (balance, open tasks, revenue this month) and must read at a glance.
Anatomy: label → value (the hero) → comparison or delta → optional tiny trend line.
Content limits:
- 3 to 4 cards in the top band (supuesto); Carbon: limit metrics, show extras on demand.
- Label 1 to 3 words. Value about 6 characters, abbreviated (12.4k) (supuesto). Delta one short phrase ("+8% vs last month").
- One dashboard serves one task (NN/g); the key KPI gets the most contrast and area (Carbon).
- No gauges or donuts (NN/g).
Actions: none inside. If it drills down, the whole card is the link with a trailing arrow bottom-right (Carbon clickable tile). Period and filter controls live once above the band.
Measures: value 32 to 40px/600, tabular figures; label 13 to 14px muted; value to label ratio about 2.5:1 (supuesto); padding 24px; 8px label to value, 8px value to delta; gap between cards 16 to 24px; minimum aspect 2:1 (Carbon); sparkline about 24px high, no axes (supuesto). White space groups and ranks (Carbon).
States:
- Rest: flat, hairline or soft surface.
- Hover/active (clickable only): subtle tint.
- Focus-visible: ring on the card.
- Loading: skeleton at final size.
- Empty: em dash plus one line ("No sales yet"), not a zero.
- Error: "Couldn't load" plus retry; no stale number.
- Stale: "Updated 2 h ago".
- Delta: sign and icon; colour never alone (NN/g).
Avoid:
- Seven KPIs of equal weight.
- Missing unit or currency.
- Green/red as the only up/down cue.
- A chart in every card.
- Layout jumping when digits grow.
